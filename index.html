About Veni Delights

Veni Delights is a premium homemade bakery based in Klang, Selangor, specialising in beautifully crafted cakes, brownies, cheese tarts, fruit tarts, egg tarts, pavlova pudding cakes, and elegant dessert corner services for every celebration.

Every dessert is freshly prepared using quality ingredients with attention to taste, presentation, and customer satisfaction. Whether it's a birthday, wedding, corporate event, or festive celebration — we create desserts that make every occasion truly memorable.

  • Homemade Daily Fresh preparation every day with no shortcuts
  • Premium Ingredients Quality-first approach in every recipe
  • Custom Designs Available Personalised for birthdays, weddings and events
  • Delivery Available Klang Valley delivery and Malaysia-wide bulk orders
