# PRODUCT EXPERIENCE REPORT — Veni Delights

**Evaluation Domain:** Frontend UI/UX, Product Presentation, Discovery Flow, and Micro-interactions  
**Author:** Creative Director & Senior UI/UX Specialist

---

## 1. Discovery Experience Architecture

The legacy website presented products in static tabular lists with basic text and standard WhatsApp redirect buttons. The new platform introduces an editorial, tactile, high-conversion discovery experience:

### Key Enhancements
1. **Interactive Category Filtering Tabs:**
   - Instant filtering across Butter Cakes, Brownies, Pandan Layer, Pavlova, Tarts, and Traditional Sweets without page reload.
   - Preserves scroll position and heading context.
2. **Editorial Food Photography Framing:**
   - 4:3 aspect-ratio image containers with subtle zoom-on-hover transforms (`transform: scale(1.06)`).
   - High dynamic range lighting, crisp shadows, and warm cream surfaces that showcase pastry texture and glaze depth.
3. **Transparent Price & Bulk Tier Display:**
   - Dedicated visual tier chips for Fruit Tarts (RM3.00 down to RM2.80 for 31+ pcs) and Cheese Tarts (RM2.50 down to RM2.00 for 101+ pcs).
   - Prominently highlights minimum order limits (10 pcs) to avoid customer friction during ordering.
4. **Order Drawer Integration (Add-to-Order):**
   - Quick "Add to Order" button overlay appearing on card hover.
   - Instant visual toast notification confirming item addition.
   - Non-disruptive cart slide-out allowing customer to review multiple items before initiating WhatsApp handoff.

## 2. Accessibility & Conversion Optimization
- **Dual Conversion Pathways:**
  - *Direct Purchase:* Instant 1-click WhatsApp order using pre-formatted product message.
  - *Multi-Product Cart:* Add multiple varieties (e.g. 1 Pure Butter Cake + 20 Strawberry Cheese Tarts) into a single unified WhatsApp order message.
- **Cognitive Clarity:**
  - Clearly differentiates fixed-price confirmed items from "Contact for Pricing" heritage items (Adhirasam, Kuih Muih).
  - Clear vegetarian badges for customer dietary reassurance.
